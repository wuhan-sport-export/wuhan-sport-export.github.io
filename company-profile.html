Company Profile

Wuhan Junji Jia Sporting Goods Store (Sole Proprietorship)

Company Overview

Business

Wuhan Junji Jia Sporting Goods Store is a sole proprietorship engaged in the wholesale and retail of sporting goods, providing export sales service to overseas buyers.

Location

Huangpi District, Wuhan, Hubei, China

Main Business

  • Fitness equipment
  • Sports accessories
  • Outdoor sports goods
  • Training supplies

Export Service

We export to buyers in the United States and other regions. We support wholesale and retail orders, accept bank transfer (including US ACH domestic transfer), and arrange international shipping.

Order Process

Orders are handled via direct communication. Customers confirm products, quantity and pricing, then make payment by bank transfer. Goods are shipped via international logistics after payment is confirmed.
